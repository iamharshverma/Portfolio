'''Harsh Verma''' is an internationally recognized Principal Software Engineer in Artificial Intelligence at [[Palo Alto Networks]], enterprise AI architect, author, keynote speaker, and computer science researcher.<ref>{{cite web |title=The Org: Harsh Verma - Principal Software Engineer in AI at Palo Alto Networks |url=https://theorg.com/org/palo-alto-networks/person/harsh-verma |website=The Org}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Wikidata Entity Q139827028: Harsh Verma |url=https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q139827028 |website=Wikidata}}</ref> He is an official member and columnist for the [[Forbes Technology Council]],<ref>{{cite web |title=Harsh Verma - Forbes Technology Council Profile |url=https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbestechcouncil/people/harshverma/ |website=Forbes}}</ref> a Senior Member of the [[Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers]] (SMIEEE), an elected Fellow of the [[British Computer Society]] (FBCS), an elected Fellow of the [[Royal Society of Arts]] (FRSA), and a Full Member of [[Sigma Xi]]: The Scientific Research Honor Society.<ref>{{cite web |title=Harsh Verma Invited Associations & Fellowships |url=https://iamharshverma.github.io/page-memberships |website=Harsh Verma Portfolio}}</ref>

Verma has authored two books on enterprise autonomous systems and cyber defense, published {{nowrap|27+}} peer-reviewed research papers across IEEE, Springer Nature, Oxford Academic, and international journals with over 150 academic citations,<ref>{{cite web |title=Harsh Verma Google Scholar Citations |url=https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=zSt9oRMAAAAJ |website=Google Scholar}}</ref> and received {{nowrap|27}} international technology and leadership honors.

== Career and Executive Leadership ==
Verma serves as Principal Software Engineer (AI) at [[Palo Alto Networks]], where he architects autonomous multi-agent systems, deterministic AI security guardrails, and high-throughput enterprise cloud platforms.<ref>{{cite web |title=The Engineer Rewriting the Rules by Thinking AI Beyond the Code: Inside the World of Harsh Verma |url=https://nyweeklyjournal.com/blog/the-engineer-rewriting-the-rules-by-thinking-%E2%80%9Cai-beyond-the-code%E2%80%9D-inside-the-world-of-harsh-verma |website=New York Weekly Journal}}</ref> Earlier in his career, he engineered large-scale distributed systems, real-time analytics pipelines, and cloud security infrastructure across enterprise technology organizations.

In addition to his engineering leadership, Verma serves as an official contributor on the [[Forbes Technology Council]], an industry awards judge for the Business Intelligence Group (BIG), Globee Awards, and Stevie Awards, and a startup mentor for [[MassChallenge]] and ADPList.
