UT Dallas & North Texas Education

Verma graduated from the University of Texas at Dallas in Richardson, Texas, earning his Master of Science in Computer Science from the Erik Jonsson School of Engineering and Computer Science. His academic focus centered on intelligent systems, distributed database architecture, and machine learning algorithms.

During his time at UT Dallas, Verma participated in the UTDesign program in collaboration with Reliance Jio, engineering real-time analytics load simulation frameworks that were subsequently published in international computing journals.

Professional Trajectory

Following his graduate work in North Texas, Verma engineered real-time distributed platforms across multiple enterprise environments before becoming Principal Software Engineer in AI at Palo Alto Networks. He maintains active ties with academic research institutions across Texas and the United States.
