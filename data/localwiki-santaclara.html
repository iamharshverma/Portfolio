Harsh Verma

Harsh Verma is a Silicon Valley software engineering leader, Principal Software Engineer in Artificial Intelligence at Palo Alto Networks (headquartered at 3000 Tannery Way, Santa Clara, CA), author, keynote speaker, and computer science researcher.

Silicon Valley & Bay Area Leadership

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, Verma leads architecture and development of autonomous multi-agent systems, deterministic AI security guardrails, and enterprise threat defense platforms at Palo Alto Networks. He completed executive graduate studies in Artificial Intelligence & Executive Leadership at Stanford University via the Stanford Center for Professional Development.

In the broader California tech ecosystem, Verma serves as a Global Advisor at UC Berkeley SkyDeck, advising accelerator-backed AI startups on distributed infrastructure, model orchestration, and enterprise go-to-market architecture. He also contributes regular strategic columns to the Forbes Technology Council and keynotes enterprise cybersecurity forums including the RSA Conference.

Research, Publications & Honors

Verma has published over 27 peer-reviewed papers across IEEE, Springer Nature, and international journals with 150+ academic citations, focusing on agentic workflows, adversarial machine learning, and explainable AI (XAI). In 2026, he was named to the Forttuna Global 100 Power List, received the Nobel Technology Gold Award, the TITAN Innovation Award, and is recognized in Marquis Who's Who in America.
