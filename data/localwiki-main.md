### Overview & Executive Engineering Leadership
Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, Harsh Verma leads the design and implementation of autonomous multi-agent systems, deterministic AI security guardrails, and enterprise threat defense platforms at **Palo Alto Networks** in Santa Clara, California. His architectural work focuses on bridging the gap between theoretical machine learning models and high-throughput, enterprise-scale production environments.

Earlier in his career, Verma engineered distributed streaming data architectures, real-time analytics platforms, and neural ranking systems across leading technology organizations including Hike Messenger, Reliance Jio (UT-Design), Worlds Enterprises, and Hypergiant Sensory Science.

Beyond his corporate engineering responsibilities, Verma serves as an official member and columnist on the **Forbes Technology Council**, a Global Advisor at **UC Berkeley SkyDeck**, an industry awards judge for the Business Intelligence Group (BIG) and Globee Awards, and an executive mentor on ADPList and MassChallenge.
