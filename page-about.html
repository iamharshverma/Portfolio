Executive Biography

About Harsh Verma

Harsh Verma is a Principal Software Engineer in AI at Palo Alto Networks, focused on advancing autonomous agent systems, multi-agent reasoning topologies, and intelligent AI strategies. Harsh is also an official Forbes Technology Council member and, as an international keynote speaker, mentor, and advisor, he brings technical depth and strategic guidance to enterprise leaders adopting next-generation AI capabilities. He drives responsible, scalable AI adoption, shaping how enterprises innovate, secure, and operationalize the next generation of intelligent systems. Harsh specializes in offering scalable AI solutions, mentoring aspiring entrepreneurs, delivering impactful conference talks, and advising startups on their AI strategies.

His contributions have been recognized through globally respected awards and institutions, reinforcing both impact and influence. He is an elected Fellow of the British Computer Society (FBCS), the UK Royal Chartered institute for IT. He is a recipient of the Global Recognition Award (2026) for measurable advances in enterprise AI architectures and autonomous cybersecurity defense, named in the Most Admire Global Indians 2026, and honored with the Globee Business Awards for innovation in global business leadership. He serves as a Senior Member of IEEE, reflecting peer-reviewed recognition of sustained technical excellence and lasting contributions to computer science and AI.

Beyond the enterprise, Harsh plays a visible role shaping the global AI & deep-tech ecosystem. Through advising and mentorship at UC Berkeley SkyDeck, judging global AI innovation forums, and publishing thought leadership in Forbes and HackerNoon, he actively contributes to defining how AI leadership evolves. His research and architectural frameworks influence not only the organizations he serves, but also the broader community of AI practitioners, autonomous agent systems, and enterprise security standards worldwide.
